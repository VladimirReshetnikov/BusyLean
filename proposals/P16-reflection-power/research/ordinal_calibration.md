P5's language with W-types at every finite type, informally
`Lambda ~= psi(Omega_omega)`.

The repository calibrates P5 near `f_(Lambda + omega^2)(42)` and Contender 6
near `f_(Lambda * Lambda)(42) = f_(Lambda^2)(42)`.  The proposed promotion
operator traverses a fresh universal W-index while each node invokes the entire
preceding scheduler.  The natural bookkeeping model is therefore

```text
alpha |-> alpha * Lambda.
```

Starting from the incumbent's `Lambda^2`, order `h` is accordingly modeled by
`Lambda^(h+2)`.  With

```text
H = succ (42 + p5Family 0 42),
```

This is deliberately labeled a calibration, not a theorem.  The archive's Lean
proofs establish strict inequalities of natural numbers without importing any
ordinal notation system or any assumptions about a fast-growing hierarchy.
